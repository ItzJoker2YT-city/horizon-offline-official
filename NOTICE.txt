NOTICE — Horizon Offline Launcher (unofficial)

This repository publishes an unofficial offline build of the Horizon launcher:
the official Horizon launcher sources with a small local patch that lets the
launcher play without a Microsoft account (Horizon's MIT license permits
building, modifying and redistributing with it).

- The launcher itself is the work of the Horizon project and its contributors:
  https://github.com/horizon-rust/horizon  (MIT, see LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)

- The offline patch was adapted from gerdaroot/Horizon-Offline-Launcher:
  https://github.com/gerdaroot/Horizon-Offline-Launcher

The game itself is NOT modified, republished or re-signed here: game versions
are downloaded from Horizon's own releases and verified by the launcher exactly
as the official launcher does.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Mojang,
Microsoft, or the Horizon project.
